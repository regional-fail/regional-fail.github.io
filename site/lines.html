How we score

Each line gets a 0–100 score over the chosen period. Rush-hour trains count double (inbound arriving 6:00–9:30am, outbound leaving 4:00–7:00pm, weekdays):

  • 50%: share of scheduled trains that actually ran. A ghost train is a total failure.
  • 30%: share of finished trips on time, by SEPTA's own rule (5 minutes late or less at the last stop).
  • 20%: how late trips ran on average, capped at 15 minutes.

Grades: A 90+, B 80+, C 70+, D 60+, F below 60. A line is ranked only with enough observed trips in the period (). Trains we couldn't observe are "unknown" and never counted against SEPTA. Through trains that cross Center City count toward each line they run on.

Data: SEPTA's public real-time feeds and published timetable.
